Program search

How to use this table

This table contains an overview of all Global Experiences programs at UCalgary. You can filter by putting anything into the search bar to see specific opportunities related to your field of study, interests or desired location.

Column descriptions

Program – Programs are named by the university. Some universities have multiple programs listed.

Type – Global Experiences are grouped into exchange, research, fee-paying, work experience and Global Classroom. 

Country – This table is organized alphabetically by country as a default. Please note: the United Kingdoms are listed as individual countries, Scotland and England.

Timeframe – This refers to how long the program is, as well as when it typically runs. Many of our partners have specific summer programs they run.

Faculty – We have many partner universities that are open to students from any faculty. Some faculties offer specific programs to their students: Haskayne, Schulich, Science and Law. You can search your faculty to filter opportunities.

Keywords – This column has relevant fields of study and information about the university that may help with your search. For example, you can search "communications" and universities who specialize in communications studies may come up. This column also has minimum GPA or year of study, if relevant. Please note, this is not an exhaustive list and there may be programs who have what you're looking for outside of these keywords.

Happy exploring!

Definitions

Exchange

Exchange programs are partnerships UCalgary has with universities all over the world. You pay your regular UCalgary tuition to study at another institution, transferring your credit back to UCalgary when you're done.

Fee-paying

Fee-paying usually refers to short-term programs where you pay the host university directly.

Semester

Also known as a "term" or "trimester", for Global Experiences, semester usually refers to exchange programs that are the length of one academic term.

Full year

Exchange programs that are a full year in length, usually two academic terms.

Short term

Short term global experiences are anything less than a semester in length, usually 4 – 6 weeks (but this varies by the program).

Summer

Summer programs take place in the Spring/Summer term and may be either exchange or fee-paying. Many universities have specific summer programs they run for international students.

GPA

When we refer to GPA, it's as a minimum requirement for the program, usually set by the host university. For example, "2.5 GPA" means that a student must have at least a 2.5 GPA at the time of applying to participate in that program. Most start at 2.5 GPA but some require as high as 3.2 GPA.

Language of instruction

Some universities offer classes only in the country's language and usually you have to meet minimum language requirements to be accepted. Some international universities offer instruction in English as well. When we tag a program with "Japanese" and "English" for example, that school offers classes in both. You can look at the program page for detailed information on requirements for language.
